**A new puzzle: Hex Islands.**

- **Hex Islands**: Islands on a honeycomb. Shade the sea around numbered islands on a hexagon of hexagonal cells: one number per island, islands never touch, all the sea joins up, and three sea cells never meet at a corner. Four sizes from side 4 to side 7, with easy, medium and hard puzzles, a daily puzzle, hints and a tutorial.

- Hints name each cell by its row number (shown at the left of every row) and its place in the row: c4 is the third cell from the left in row 4.
