**A new Origami theme, and every theme looks more like itself.**

- **Origami** replaces Low-poly: each square is a sheet of paper folded on the diagonal, and stars, islands and lines lift off the page on soft shadows. Light and dark.
- **Theme fonts everywhere**: buttons, menus, chips, dialogs and hints now use the theme's font, with a plainer companion font for small labels so they stay easy to read.
- **Pencil**: bold lines and outlines waver and press like a real pencil, dots are hand-made marks, and numbers sit in the middle of their circles (in every handwritten font).
- **Paper cut** has a faint paper-fibre texture; **Blueprint** shades with a soft wash on grainy drawing paper instead of diagonal lines.
