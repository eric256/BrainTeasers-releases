**Boards shaped like your phone, and levels to unlock.**

- **Phone-shaped boards** for Pipes, Link Up, Light Up, Rectangles, Bridges, Trail, Slitherlink, Islands, Pearls, Panda Pairs, Sun & Moon and Minesweeper: sizes like 7×11 and 9×14 that fill the screen. Square sizes are still there, and every tall puzzle still has exactly one solution.
- **Unlock as you play**: each puzzle starts with its smallest Easy board. Solve 3 at a level to open the next difficulty and the next size up. Daily puzzles count, and anything you've played before stays open. When a solve unlocks something, the finishing screen offers to play it. The home screen shows how far along each puzzle you are. Prefer everything open? Profile & settings → Unlock every size and difficulty.
- **Daily puzzles have no hints**, so everyone's daily time is their own.
- **Highlight mistakes** has moved to the new ⋮ puzzle menu (with How to play) and to each puzzle's menu.
- **Icons on the buttons**: Undo, Hint, Clear, Lock and the rest.
