The first public release of Brain Teasers.

**Nine puzzles:** Panda Pairs, Sudoku, Star Battle, Nonogram, Sun & Moon, Slitherlink, Trail, Minesweeper and Lights Out — each freshly generated with exactly one solution.

- Daily puzzles that everyone shares, with streaks and history
- Written hints that teach the next logical step
- 300 hand-drawn nonogram pictures to collect in a gallery
- Share any puzzle with a code or link and challenge friends to beat your time
- Favorites, list or tile view, and light/dark mode
- Updates itself from this page: you'll be offered new versions as they're released

Install: download **BrainTeasers-1.4.0.apk** below on your Android phone (Android 8.0+) and open it.
If you installed a test build before, uninstall it first.
