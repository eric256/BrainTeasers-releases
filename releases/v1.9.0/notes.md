**Three new puzzles, a tougher Link Up and smarter hints.**

New puzzles:
- **Towers**: fill the grid with towers of different heights; the numbers around the edge say how many you can see from that side.
- **Islands**: shade the sea so every number sits on its own island of that size, with one connected sea and no 2×2 pools.
- **Pearls**: draw one loop through every pearl, going straight through white pearls and turning on black ones.

Also new:
- **Link Up Expert**: barriers between some squares and fewer, longer lines.
- Share past puzzles: puzzles you solve from now on get a share button in History & best times, so you can send them to a friend with your time to beat.
- Hints now recharge: after using one, the Hint button counts down 10 seconds before it's ready again.
- Hit a mine? Minesweeper now shows the move that would have avoided it, and taking the move back highlights it on the board.
- Waiting for a big puzzle? The loading spinner now keeps you company with a few silly lines.
- Big Minesweeper boards drag in any direction at once, including diagonally, and glide when you flick them.

Nonograms and Lights Out have been retired. Your history for them is kept, and their share codes now explain that they're gone.
