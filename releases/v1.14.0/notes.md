**Better-balanced puzzles.**

- **Pipes**: Easy, Medium and Hard are now clearly different on every size, including 5×5.
- **Rectangles**: Hard is reliably harder than Medium, including on the 6×9 board.
- **Link Up 9×13 Hard**: fewer, longer lines, as tough as 9×9 Hard.
- **Phone-shaped boards on smaller phones** shrink to fit, so the whole board and its buttons are on screen.
- **Tablets and foldables**: the home screen's tile view fits more puzzles across (up to six) with icons at a comfortable size.
- **Wide screens**: a puzzle's page splits into two columns (playing on the left; how to play and settings on the right), and while playing side by side a hint opens under the buttons instead of across the bottom.
- **Change the look from anywhere**: the palette button on the home screen and each puzzle's menu, or ⋮ → Appearance while playing, opens the themes and light/dark without leaving your puzzle.
