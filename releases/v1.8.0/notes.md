**Choose your board size and difficulty.**

- Every new puzzle now has two choices: the board size and how hard it is (Easy, Medium or Hard). Sudoku is always 9×9, so it just has a difficulty; nonograms are pictures, so they just have sizes.
- Harder means more thinking, not just a bigger board: hard puzzles need deeper reasoning, while easy ones can be solved one simple step at a time.
- New sizes: 10×10 Panda Pairs, 5×5 Star Battle and 6×6 Trail.
- Your last choice is remembered for each game, and best times are kept for every size and difficulty.
- Each puzzle's menu now fits on one screen: today's puzzle and the new-puzzle picker up front, the tutorial behind the 🎓 button, and a "History & best times" page with your best time for every size and difficulty.
- A new profile button at the top of the home screen opens your stats, the light/dark theme, the list or tile layout, release notes ("What's new"), update checks and licenses. It's also where Google Play Games sign-in will go.
- You'll see these notes after each update; read them again any time from "What's new" in the profile menu.

Shared puzzle codes now include the difficulty. Codes shared from older versions still open exactly as they were.
