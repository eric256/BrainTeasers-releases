**Four more puzzles on a honeycomb, and new looks.**

- **Hex Minesweeper**: numbers count up to six neighbours. Still no guessing ever needed, and the biggest boards pan and zoom.
- **Hex Link Up**: join the pairs with lines that can turn six ways.
- **Hex Trail**: one numbered path through every cell, the way this puzzle was first invented.
- **Hex Bridges**: bridges run along three directions, so islands can link up at 60°.

**New looks:** pick a theme in Profile & settings → Appearance.
- **Pencil**: graphite on paper, with sketched lines, cross-hatched shading and handwritten numbers, like the puzzle books.
- **Neon**: glowing lines on a midnight board.
- **High contrast**: bold, crisp boards with colour-blind-friendly colours.
- **Classic** is the look you know. Every theme works with light and dark (Neon is always dark).

Each new hex puzzle comes with sizes, Easy, Medium and Hard, a tutorial, daily puzzles, share codes and step-by-step hints. Cells are named the same way in every hex game: c4 is the third cell from the left in row 4.
