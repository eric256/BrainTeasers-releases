**Four new puzzles and a tutorial for every game.**

New puzzles:
- **Light Up** — place bulbs to light every square without bulbs seeing each other.
- **Link Up** — join matching numbers with lines that fill the whole grid.
- **Bridges** — connect every island with the right number of bridges.
- **Rectangles** — split the grid into rectangles whose areas match their numbers.

Each comes with daily puzzles, streaks, sharing codes and teaching hints.

Tutorials:
- Every game now has a **Learn to play** walkthrough on its menu: three illustrated pages, then a guided puzzle where a coach shows each next step. Tutorials don't affect your streaks or history.

Also: clearer Link Up rules, and the Light Up tagline no longer suggests using as few bulbs as possible.
