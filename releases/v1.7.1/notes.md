**Board coordinates, tougher puzzles and friendlier tutorials.**

Coordinates:
- Every board now has letters along the top and numbers down the side, and hints name squares like a chessboard ("the 2 at h6") so they're easier to follow.
- On huge Minesweeper boards the labels stay pinned to the edges while you scroll.

Harder levels:
- Medium and hard puzzles are now picked by how much real reasoning they need, so they are genuinely harder, not just bigger.
- Panda Pairs and Trail get much tougher hard puzzles and bigger boards.

Tutorials:
- You can close or skip a tutorial at any point and restart it later from the game's menu.

Puzzles shared from older versions still open exactly as they were.
