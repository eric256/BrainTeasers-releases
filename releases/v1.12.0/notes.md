**A new puzzle, six new looks, and zoom.**

- **Pipes**: turn the tiles so every pipe joins one network with no loops. Water flows from the tap in the middle as you connect tiles. Tap to turn a tile, hold to lock it. Sizes 5×5 to 11×11, with Easy, Medium and Hard, a tutorial, daily puzzles and step-by-step hints.

**Six new themes** in Profile & settings → Appearance, each restyling every board and the puzzle icons:
- **Blueprint**: white technical lines on blueprint blue (blue on white in light mode).
- **Low-poly**: faceted, geometric shading.
- **Chalkboard**: chalk on a slate-green board.
- **Retro pixel**: chunky pixels and an 8-bit palette.
- **Paper cut**: layers of cut paper with soft shadows.
- **Watercolour**: soft washes of paint on textured paper.

**Also new:**
- **Pinch to zoom** on every board: two fingers zoom and move around, one finger still plays. On big Minesweeper boards, pinch as well as drag.
- **Tablets and foldables**: turn them sideways for a side-by-side layout, with the board on the left and the controls beside it. Phones stay in portrait.
- **Favourites**: the ☆ is now on each puzzle's menu and play screen too.
- Cards and dialogs follow the theme's colours.
