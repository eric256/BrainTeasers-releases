# FAQ

**Does every puzzle have a solution? Only one?**
Yes. Each puzzle is generated on your phone, and a built-in solver checks it has exactly one solution
before you see it. You never have to guess (Minesweeper included).

**Do I need an internet connection?**
No. Everything works offline. The app only goes online to check for updates and download them.

**Why is my friend's puzzle different from mine?**
Puzzle codes always rebuild the exact same puzzle. If you both chose "new puzzle", you got different
random ones: share the code instead (the share button while playing).

**The app says a code is from a newer version.**
Update the app (profile button → **Check for updates**); the puzzle needs the newer puzzle maker.

**What happened to Nonograms and Lights Out?**
They were retired in version 1.9.0 to make room for Towers, Islands and Pearls. Your history for them is
still saved on your phone, and their old codes explain that they're gone.

**Do hints affect my best times?**
A puzzle solved with hints is marked "with hints" and doesn't set a best time. It still counts for your
streak.

**How do streaks work?**
Each puzzle has its own streak: solve at least one puzzle of that game (daily or not) on consecutive days.
If you haven't played yet today, yesterday's streak stays alive until midnight.

**Will my progress transfer to a new phone?**
Not yet: history is stored on the phone. Cloud saves are planned with Google Play Games.

**Android says the app could be harmful / blocks the install.**
That's the standard warning for apps installed outside the Play Store. See
[Is the download genuine?](../README.md#is-the-download-genuine) to check the file, then allow the
install. See [Installing](README.md#installing).

**How do I uninstall?**
Long-press the app icon → **Uninstall**. This also deletes your history.
