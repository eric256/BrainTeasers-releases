## Installing

Brain Teasers isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install it straight from this page.

1. On your Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer), open the
   [latest release](https://github.com/eric256/BrainTeasers-releases/releases/latest).
2. Under **Assets**, tap the file named `BrainTeasers-x.y.z.apk` to download it.
3. Open the download (from the notification, or your **Files** / **Downloads** app).
4. The first time, Android says your browser or files app isn't allowed to install apps. Tap
   **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
5. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

Play Protect may ask you to confirm an app from an unknown developer; choose **Install anyway**.

## Updating

The app checks this page for a new version about once a day. When there is one, it shows what's new and
offers **Update**: it downloads the new version and Android asks you to confirm the install. You can also
choose **Later**, or **Skip this version**.

To check yourself, tap the profile button at the top right of the home screen, then **Check for updates**.

After an update, the app shows what's new once. To read the notes for every version again, open the
profile menu and tap **What's new**.

Your history, streaks and settings are kept when you update.
